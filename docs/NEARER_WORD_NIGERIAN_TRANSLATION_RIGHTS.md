# NeaRer Word — Nigerian Scripture rights register

**Status: discovery only; no Nigerian translation is cleared for redistribution in NeaRer.**

## What the publisher currently publishes

The Bible Society of Nigeria (BSN) states that it has complete Bibles in 27 Nigerian languages, with additional translation/revision projects underway. Its public Bible-download page lists 17 language/edition rows on YouVersion, including the separate Hausa Ajami edition and an Eggon New Testament. A newer BSN guide describes 18 Nigerian languages available in YouVersion and names some different language entries. These are not interchangeable claims: a language may have a complete Bible, a New Testament, a portion, or a project in progress.

Official references:
- BSN Bible-download page and its 17 edition rows: https://www.biblesociety-nigeria.org/bible-downloads/
- BSN newer guide reporting 18 Nigerian-language editions: https://www.biblesociety-nigeria.org/download-install-and-read-the-bible-in-your-local-language/

## Required written permission before NeaRer distribution

For each exact edition, request written confirmation of:

1. The legal copyright owner and authorised licensor.
2. Permission to reproduce the complete text or named portions inside NeaRer.
3. Permission for offline downloads, local device storage, backups and redistribution through NeaRer's own content-pack service.
4. Whether commercial use, advertising, donations or paid app features are permitted.
5. Permitted territories, languages/scripts, version names, logos and trademark usage.
6. Required copyright notices, attribution wording, publisher links and in-app placement.
7. Whether text may be normalised for search, punctuation, verse segmentation, typography or accessibility; retain the source text and document every permitted transformation.
8. Whether word-level original-language tagging, parallel display, audio pairing or derivative study metadata are permitted.
9. Any expiry, update, reporting, download-count or takedown obligations.
10. Whether a signed agreement or per-edition approval is required from a translation committee or partner publisher in addition to BSN.

Do not scrape YouVersion content, bypass app controls, copy downloaded files from a third-party app, or infer a redistribution licence from the fact that a translation is free to download for personal use.

## NeaRer pack publication gates

An edition may move from PERMISSION_REQUIRED to APPROVED_FOR_DISTRIBUTION only after a written agreement is archived and reviewed. The pack manifest must then record:
- stable translation/edition ID and language tag;
- actual coverage and canon (full Bible, NT, or portion);
- publisher, rights holder, source and exact version;
- licence/permission reference and date;
- required attribution and copyright notice;
- permitted territories, commercial use and offline caching terms;
- SHA-256, pack version and source-text provenance.

The app must continue to display "permission required" until every gate is satisfied.
